Who We Are

A luxury embroidery & monogram brand.

KAS THREADZ is dedicated to precision craftsmanship. We create premium, detailed embroidery and personalised monogram designs for fashion houses, corporate brands, tailors, traditional wear and high-end apparel.

Our signature style blends cultural depth, artistic creativity and meticulous stitching. Whether it's a single monogram or a fully embellished agbada set, we treat every thread as part of a larger story — one worn with pride.

Our Vision

To become Africa's leading luxury embroidery and monogram brand — known for precision, cultural artistry and world-class craftsmanship.

Our Mission

To elevate personal and brand identity through high-quality embroidery and monogram creations that reflect excellence, heritage and style.

Our Identity

From logo to thread, every element has meaning.

Our brand identity is built on a deep philosophy of innovation and excellence. The needle and thread woven through our mark represent the craft itself; the layered lines echo the structure of fine fabric.

Our palette — deep burgundy, warm taupe, soft ivory and true black — reflects heritage, warmth and refinement. These colours run through everything we make, keeping the KAS THREADZ signature unmistakable.
